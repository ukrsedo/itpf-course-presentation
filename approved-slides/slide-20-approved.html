Session 1 Setting the Scene

Bimodal Procurement by the Asian Development Bank (ADB)

Mode logic

Conventional procurement processes prioritising compliance, risk mitigation and cost efficiency.

Standardised procedures and documentation keep procurement aligned with ADB regulations and policies.

Scope

Predefined goods and services: standard equipment, supplies and software licences that do not require design or customisation.

Non-complex procurements: lower-value, low-risk contracts following routine bidding procedures.

Open competitive bidding and alternative arrangements: packaged for competition, with flexibility to use borrower procedures if aligned with ADB standards.

Consultants and non-consulting services: standard IT, maintenance or facility services.

Mode logic

Flexible, agile and innovation-oriented procurement for complex project requirements and emerging market trends.

The model explores new procurement methods, digital technologies and sustainable practices.

Scope

Digital Sandbox Programs: engagement with startups and technology companies to test and co-create solutions.

Knowledge sharing and capacity building: workshops on innovative procurement practices, especially in infrastructure and digital transformation.

Pilot projects and PPP models: novel solutions such as smart infrastructure or green technologies.

Policy and advisory work: guidelines and policy notes encouraging procurement to stimulate innovation.
