Game #1

SAP Transformation
Case setup
You are the customer negotiation team. Your task is to protect continuity, control cost, and manage the supplier’s transformation agenda.
Expect pressure around migration timing, commercial concessions, cloud roadmap, and end-of-life risk.

How to play

1

Read the brief

Review customer objectives, supplier objectives, constraints, and the opening context.

2

Negotiate in rounds

Select actions carefully. Each round changes leverage, risk, and commercial position.

3

Watch active effects

Supplier moves can block options, create urgency, or change the economics.

4

Commit or withdraw

Final outcome depends on trade-offs, not on always “winning” every move.
