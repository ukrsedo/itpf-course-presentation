Session 4 Sourcing of IT Services

Case #4

Contact Center Renewal Negotiations
Case setup
A state-owned company must renew a contact center contract while balancing strict SLA requirements, cost control, informal supplier arrangements, and tier-based pricing.
The supplier is trying to recover account acquisition investment through extra charges.

Your negotiation focus

1

SLA

Protect legally required response performance without overbuying capacity.

2

Contract compliance

Challenge tier allocation and informal deviations from the contract.

3

Resource allocation

Test whether agents, team leaders and quality resources match real demand.

4

Rates

Negotiate rate logic, extra charges, and the supplier’s recovery narrative.
