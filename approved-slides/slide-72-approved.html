How to read it

The matrix links sourcing strategy to two questions: business importance and supply-market complexity.

1
Profit impactCost, value contribution, business criticality, and operational exposure.
2
Supply riskScarcity, switching barriers, technology pace, logistics complexity, and supplier power.
3
Sourcing responseEfficiency, market leverage, risk protection, or strategic partnership.
