FSE Workers Calculation

FSE Workers are calculated by categorising each Customer Worker to one of the worker categories below, multiplying the Worker number by the applicable percentage rate, and then adding totals for each category of worker.
Worker count × applicable percentage = FSE Worker Calculation

Reduction caps

Ay. FSE Reduction Option Part 1.
The decreases in the FSE Worker count under this subsection 5(a) may not exceed a decrease of nine percent (9%) of the initial maximum FSE Worker count in any single annual reporting period, and the maximum total FSE Worker count decrease may not result in an adjusted maximum FSE Worker count that is less than eighty two percent (82%) of the initial maximum FSE Worker count during the order term. Any future fees associated with the reduced FSE Workers will be reduced from future invoices. If the fees associated with the reduced FSE Workers have been prepaid an unearned, a credit for the reduced fees will be applied only to subsequent invoice for the subscription service...

Commercial meaning

The right to reduce is not an unlimited scale-down right. It is an annual adjustment mechanism constrained by a yearly reduction cap and a floor for the whole order term.
