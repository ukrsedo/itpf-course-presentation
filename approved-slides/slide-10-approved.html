Mitigation requires controls across demand, licensing, adoption and consumption.

1

Review adoption regularly

Compare assigned licences with active usage and remove shelfware.

2

Track committed benefits

Separate measurable legacy benefits from uncertain innovation value.

3

Segment licences by role

Allocate premium functionality to roles that genuinely need it.

4

Use trials and entitlements first

Test demand before scaling and use included features before add-ons.

5

Control experimental licences

Define approval, ownership, review dates and exit conditions.

6

Deploy quotas and caps

Set consumption thresholds, alerts and budget limits.

7

Use bundles selectively

Choose bundles only where the included products are actually used.

8

Manage change and adoption

Link rollout, training and ownership to realised business outcomes.
