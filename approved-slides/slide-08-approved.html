Software Inflation and Shelfware

↑

Software Inflation

The progressive growth of an organization’s software estate—in terms of applications, subscriptions, licenses, and consumption-based services—leading to rising costs, increasing complexity, and diminishing cost efficiency.

∅

Shelfware

The software or digital licenses that an organization pays for but never actively installs, uses, or fully adopts.

4%–18%

Software spend spreads from 4% to 18% of an average company's revenue, depending on the industry.
