Session 1 Setting the Scene

Concepts for IT Transformation

From products and digital tools to service value and bimodal delivery
Servitization
Definition
The transformation of a business from selling products to offering integrated products and services, creating added value and long-term customer relationships.
Procurement implication
Buying shifts from asset ownership to outcomes, continuity, availability and provider capability.
Digitalization Paradox
Definition
The phenomenon where organizations invest in advanced digital technologies expecting improved performance, efficiency and competitive advantage but fail to realize those benefits, or even experience setbacks.
Why it happens
Digital initiatives create unanticipated complexity or operational disruption when processes, skills, governance and culture do not change with the technology.
Bi-modal IT
Definition
An approach where an organization manages two separate modes of IT operations:
  1. Mode 1 focuses on stability and efficiency, managing traditional IT systems with predictable processes.
  2. Mode 2 emphasizes agility and innovation, enabling faster experimentation and adaptation to change.
