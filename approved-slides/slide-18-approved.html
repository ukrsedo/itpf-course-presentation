Reliability vs Agility

Bimodal operations: plan-driven stability and iterative change
Reliability
Plan-driven
Stable regulated mission environment
Well-defined requirements based on established technology
Established and diverse supply base
Homogeneous stakeholders
Formal documented relationships
Cost and reliability predominance, low risk appetite
Agility
Iterative change-driven
Fast-changing business or customer environment
Evolving requirements and emerging technologies
Exploratory or fragmented supply base
Multiple stakeholders with changing priorities
Collaborative working and short feedback cycles
Value, speed and learning predominance, higher risk appetite
