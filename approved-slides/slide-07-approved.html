2025–2026 SaaS Market Trends and Challenges

$316bn → $1.5tn
SaaS market: $316bn in 2025, up from $266bn in 2024 (+19% YoY), and projected to reach $1.5tn by 2034.
$20.3bn → $91.2bn
AI-as-a-Service market from 2025 to 2030, projected at 35% YoY growth.
+75% YoY
Growth in spending on AI-native apps, where AI is the core functionality.
9% EBITDA
Median SaaS EBITDA margin in 2025, with 26% growth; median net margin 1.5–2% and gross margins around 80%.
Rule of 40
EBITDA + growth ≥40% indicates a healthy balance of growth and profitability.
Business ownership
