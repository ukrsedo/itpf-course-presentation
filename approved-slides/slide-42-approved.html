Session 3 Cloud Computing

Buy or Build?

COTS, custom build, or customise: the sourcing decision changes cost, risk and delivery model.

Core sourcing choice

BUYCommercial off-the-shelf (COTS) product or cloud service
or
BUILDSoftware development lifecycle and internal / outsourced delivery

Decision framing

COTS / SaaS

Use whenrequirements fit market capability.
Procurement focuslicense model, data terms, SLA, support, exit rights.
Main riskconfiguration limits, lock-in, shelfware.

Customise

Use whenstandard solution needs material adaptation.
Procurement focusscope control, change requests, IP, acceptance criteria.
Main riskcost creep, upgrade friction, bespoke dependency.

Build

Use whencapability is differentiating or market fit is poor.
Procurement focusdelivery method, team capability, roadmap, ownership.
Main riskdelivery uncertainty, technical debt, maintenance burden.
SDLC stages to consider in build / customise decisions
Plan
Define
Design
Build
Test
Deploy
Discussion: IT Procurement is not only about buying. Key decision is whether to buy, build, or customise — and who carries lifecycle risk.
