Session 5 Source-to-Contract

Audit response as an evidence and governance process

An audit is partly a negotiation, but the buyer first needs a reliable position on the contractual obligation and the evidence.

The interesting questions are:

  • What exactly is being requested, and under which agreement?
  • Which entities, products, and periods are in scope?
  • Who validates technical data before it is disclosed?
  • Which findings are supported, disputed or unresolved?
  • Does the final settlement adequately close the identified issue?
