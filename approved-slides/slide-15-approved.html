Session 1 · Setting the Scene

Digitalization: Technology ≠ Transformation

Digitization

The process of converting information or physical objects into a digital format.

→

Digitalization

The use of digital technologies to transform existing business processes, services or products.

Digitization records information digitally. Digitalization changes processes and workflows to improve how the organization operates.

The paradox

Adopting AI, IoT, ERP or other digital tools does not guarantee better outcomes.

Without corresponding changes in processes, skills, governance and culture, technology can remain underused or become disruptive.

Source note: Financial Times Partner Content by TeamViewer cites McKinsey that 70% of digital transformation initiatives fail to meet their objectives and cites Gartner that only 48% meet or exceed their targets.

The article argues that transformation failure is usually an execution problem, not a vision problem. Its main practical point is that proactive IT support, monitoring and employee enablement are needed to prevent disruption, workarounds and poor adoption.
