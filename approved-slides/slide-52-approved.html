How can procurement control token waste?

Apply the Technology Procurement Framework from slide 50. Focus on where waste occurs, which control addresses it, and who should own that control.

Discussion task

  • Identify where unnecessary token consumption can occur.
  • Select the most effective demand-management or optimization control.
  • Explain who should own each control: procurement, IT, finance, or the user.

Demand controls

Quotas, approval thresholds, budgets, alerts, and team-level showback or chargeback.

Optimization controls

Model-to-task matching, shorter prompts, trimmed context, caching, batching, and removal of unnecessary calls.

Your discussion notes

Record two or three controls and identify the owner. This is not a scored activity.
