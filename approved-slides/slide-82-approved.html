Session 5 Source-to-Contract

ACV vs. TCV in SaaS Contracts

TCV shows the full commitment; ACV is that value averaged across the contract years
ACV — Annual Contract Value
The total contract value averaged over the committed contract years.
ACV = TCV ÷ contract term in years
Time horizon
One average contract year
Best for
Comparing contracts with different durations and setting annual sales targets
Shows
Average annual contract value
Does not show
Total commitment or when payments occur
Example: £300,000 TCV over 3 years → ACV = £100,000.
TCV — Total Contract Value
The total value of the contract over its full committed term.
TCV = total committed contract charges
Time horizon
Full contract term
Best for
Approvals, total spend exposure and understanding the full commitment
Shows
Total nominal contract value
Does not show
Annualised value or the time value of money
Example: a 3-year contract worth £300,000 → TCV = £300,000.
1

Comparable deal size

ACV places one-, three- and five-year contracts on the same annual basis.

2

Recurring and consumption economics

ACV became prominent through recurring subscription economics and is increasingly used to normalise consumption-based contracts.

3

Annual revenue management

ACV supports recurring-revenue reporting, annual sales targets, renewals and account planning.

4

Less distortion from term length

TCV can appear larger simply because the contract lasts longer; ACV isolates the annual average.

5

Growth and account quality

Higher ACV can signal stronger pricing, expansion or larger annual customer value.

6

Buyer risk

ACV can make a large multi-year exposure look smaller, so procurement must still distinguish committed TCV from forecast TCV.

Why ACV follows the SaaS model
ACV became prominent through recurring subscription economics and is increasingly used to normalise consumption-based contracts.
Committed TCV is the fixed multi-year obligation already accepted by the buyer.
Forecast TCV estimates total spend where users, transactions, storage, compute or API consumption can vary.
ACV provides the annualised view used for comparison, revenue planning, renewals and account management.
Procurement should review ACV together with committed or forecast TCV. NPV remains a separate calculation based on payment timing.
