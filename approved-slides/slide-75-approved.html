Session 5 Source-to-Contract

Principal–Agent Problem: Aligning Incentives

Scenario

The company hires an IT consultant to select a supplier. The consultant receives a fixed fee of £20,000.

A
Select Supplier AThe company generates £200,000 of additional business benefit.
B
Select Supplier BThe company suffers a £50,000 business loss, while the consultant receives a £40,000 supplier commission.
Information asymmetry creates moral hazard: under the basic contract, the consultant earns more by selecting Supplier B.

Payoffs after consultant compensation

Company values are net payoffs: business outcome less fees paid to the consultant.

1. Basic contractSupplier B pays the consultant more: £60k versus £20k.
2. Performance contractSupplier A now pays the consultant more: £70k versus £60k.
3. Aligned outcomeThe consultant’s best response now supports the company’s objective.
Game-theory lesson: the performance-based contract changes the consultant’s rational choice from Supplier B (£60k) to Supplier A (£70k), aligning the agent’s interests with the principal’s objective.
