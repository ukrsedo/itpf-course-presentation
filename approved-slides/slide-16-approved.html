Session 1 Setting the Scene

Technology Cost: Manage Demand Before You Negotiate

The same procurement logic applies across Cloud, SaaS and AI.
Demand Management
Commercial Negotiation
Cost Management
What are we using, spending and owning?
  • Create visibility of demand and spend
  • Allocate cost to owners and use cases
  • Set budgets, thresholds and forecasts
  • Track usage, renewal and consumption trends
Outcome: controlled and understood demand
Cost Optimization
Can we meet the same need with less?
  • Remove unused or duplicated demand
  • Right-size capacity, licences or model usage
  • Match service level to actual business need
  • Change technical or consumption choices where justified
Outcome: efficient demand baseline
Commercial Levers
How do we buy the optimized demand better?
  • Negotiate price and discount structure
  • Set commitment and volume flexibility
  • Control support, add-ons and future services
  • Protect exit, data rights and renewal position
Outcome: better economics and lower commercial risk
First understand the demand. Then optimize it. Only then negotiate the deal.
