Session 1 · Setting the Scene

3 Key Themes Shaping IT Procurement (2025–2027)

From SaaS market evidence to procurement implications
1

SaaS Cost and Governance Management

  • SaaS waste — shelfware, redundant apps and multichannel spend — remains material, while AI-driven consumption-based models emerge and dominate the market.
  • Shadow IT and decentralised IT demand undermine control, while Shadow IT shifts towards vibe coding.
  • Massive digital investments require strict ROI discipline.
2

SaaS Vendors’ Drive to Improve Margins and EBITDA

  • SaaS inflation and auto-renewals.
  • Changes in licensing models.
  • Unexpected costs and bill shock.
3

Shift to AI-Native SaaS Models and PaaS-centric Business Models

  • AI app and feature spend skyrockets.
  • In the face of vibe coding, there’s a shift from end-user solutions to service orchestration and integration.
  • AI functionality is becoming the decisive factor for supplier/solution selection.
  • IT procurement skills, practice and governance in the AI sub-category are still being established.
