1. Economic exchange

Two parties exchange something of value: goods, services or rights in return for payment or another benefit. Each party takes on obligations and expects a benefit from the exchange.

A transaction is the transfer that puts the exchange into effect. Arranging and governing that transfer requires work before and after agreement.

2. Activities needed to organise the exchange → 3. Associated transaction costs

Type of activityWhat the parties doCosts incurred

Policing and enforcementCheck that obligations are met; manage deviations; resolve disputes and enforce remedies.Monitoring, audits, performance reviews, dispute handling and legal action.

Transaction costs are the resources spent organising and governing the exchange. They sit alongside the cost of producing or delivering what is exchanged. Internal delivery also incurs coordination and control costs.
