Payment follows a verified customer result. API calls, messages and tokens measure consumption. A resolved issue or an accepted qualified lead can be an outcome.

1. Define what earns payment

Agree the result, quality threshold, baseline and exclusions. Specify treatment of failures, human handoffs, duplicates and reopened cases.

2. Prove the outcome occurred

Agree the system of record, attribution and verification window. Give the buyer access to the evidence behind each billable event.

3. Set the full commercial model

Assess the rate per result, platform and onboarding fees, minimum commitments and caps. If credits are used, make their monetary value explicit.

4. Control bills and disputes

Require spend alerts, overage rules and invoice reconciliation. Agree correction or reversal of disputed outcomes, and what happens when a cap is reached.

Support example: agree payment for a verified resolution without human intervention, after the agreed observation window. Charging for an AI reply alone measures activity.
