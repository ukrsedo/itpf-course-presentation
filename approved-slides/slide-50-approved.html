Calculated conclusions

Expansion costs 51% less per $1 of added ARR than new acquisition. Negotiate expansion rates before dependence grows.

Vertical SaaS generates 5.6× lifetime value per acquisition dollar, versus 4.1× for horizontal SaaS. The 37% higher ratio indicates stronger supplier unit economics. It does not establish customer value, profitability or discount capacity.

Payback is 11% shorter; blended CAC is 7% lower. Improved sales efficiency strengthens the case for challenging acquisition-cost arguments.

Rule of 40 improved 10 points, but remains 15 points short. High gross margins alone do not establish profitability or resilience.
