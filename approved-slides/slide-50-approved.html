Session 4 Emerging Technology & AI

Technology Procurement Framework — AI

AI demand can grow quickly because both usage and unit economics are variable.
Demand Management
Commercial Negotiation
Cost Management
Make AI demand visible and attributable
  • Inventory use cases, users, models and workload owners
  • Track token, request, compute and storage consumption
  • Set quotas, approval thresholds, budgets and alerts
  • Allocate cost through team-level showback or chargeback
Toolkit: usage telemetry • quotas • approval thresholds • budgets/alerts • showback/chargeback
Cost Optimization
Use the least-cost approach that still works
  • Match the model to task complexity
  • Shorten prompts and trim unnecessary context
  • Cache repeated outputs and batch/reuse requests where appropriate
  • Remove unnecessary calls before scaling the workflow
Toolkit: model-to-task routing • prompt/context control • caching • batching • call elimination
Commercial Levers
Protect economics as AI usage evolves
  • Negotiate token / unit pricing and volume tiers
  • Challenge committed spend and preserve flexibility between models
  • Cover future models, support and professional services
  • Protect data use, ownership, IP position, portability and exit rights
Toolkit: unit rates • commitments • future-model terms • data / IP • exit rights
Do not negotiate large AI commitments against an immature use-case and consumption forecast.
